# Changelog

- **1.2.8** — Save this story: the whole story (or the page) as one offline file. Tap to scroll can use left half = down, right half = back.
- **1.2.7** — Installs and updates from this GitHub repo.
- **1.2.6** — Nods no longer come out reversed after a few head turns. Nodding and Turning your head each offer: read on one way, the other way, change chapter, or nothing.
- **1.2.5** — Nods still register with a hand or toothbrush in front of the face. Reversed nods. Head turns can scroll.
- **1.2.4** — Smooth Auto-scroll at slow speeds. Choose where the button sits and how see-through it is.
- **1.2.3** — Auto-scroll and Tap to scroll run silently: no bar, no messages.
- **1.2.2** — Tap to scroll can use left half = back, right half = down.
- **1.2.1** — Warns when an older copy is also installed, shows its version, reports errors.
- **1.2.0** — Tap to scroll (quarter, half or full page). Auto-scroll moves to exact positions, with three faster speeds.
- **1.1.0** — Works on every site, not just FanFiction.net.
- **1.0.4** — Nod detection uses the 3D head angle: works near or far, and with a toothbrush.
- **1.0.3** — Nod detection follows the real face shape and ignores blinks.
- **1.0.2** — Fixed starting position and a Reset button.
- **1.0.0** — First version: Nod to scroll and Auto-scroll on FanFiction.net.
